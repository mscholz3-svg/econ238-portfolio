Trending now · National parks
NP
Definitely Not the Park Service
@BelovedAndUnderfunded · 14h
Proposal: Give tribes removed from national park land real ownership or management authority.
The Strongest Case
Historical connection, local knowledge, tribal jobs, tourism revenue, and actual authority rather than symbolism.
The Strongest Concerns
Public access, funding, entrance prices, management capacity, and several tribes claiming the same land.
EX
Economics Experiment
@TryItAndMeasureIt · 15h
Start with shared tribal and federal management at several parks for five years. Give tribes real authority and a share of park revenue.
Measure: environmental conditions, maintenance costs, public access, visitor prices, tribal employment, tribal revenue, and visitor satisfaction. Compare the results with similar federally managed parks.
Why this mattersA pilot turns an argument over assumptions into a test that can produce evidence.

Before you repost, run the economic test

Who controls it?Who can make the final decision?
Who benefits?Who receives revenue, land value, or better conditions?
Who pays?Who carries the cost, risk, or lost opportunity?
What changes?Does behavior change, or only the language?
“Acknowledgment may be a useful beginning, but show me the changed incentive, transferred resource, or measurable result before calling it justice.”
